Character: Batman (DC Comics)

Details:
Real Name: Bruce Wayne
Origin: As a child, Bruce Wayne witnessed the murder of his wealthy parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, in a street robbery. He swore an oath to rid his city of the evil that took their lives. He spent years traveling the world, training himself to physical and intellectual perfection and mastering various skills like martial arts, criminology, and detective work.
Abilities: Peak human physical and mental condition, master martial artist and hand-to-hand combatant, genius-level intellect, master detective, expert strategist, utilizes vast wealth to fund advanced gadgets, vehicles (Batmobile), and technology (Batsuit). Operates primarily in Gotham City.
Affiliations: Justice League, Bat-Family (Robin, Batgirl, etc.), Wayne Enterprises CEO (publicly).

Brief:
Batman is the secret identity of Bruce Wayne, a billionaire who fights crime in Gotham City. Driven by the murder of his parents, he uses his intellect, fighting skills, and advanced technology, rather than superpowers, to instill fear in criminals and protect the innocent.
