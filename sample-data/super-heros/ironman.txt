Character: Iron Man (Marvel Comics)

Details:
Real Name: Anthony Edward "Tony" Stark
Origin: A billionaire industrialist, genius inventor, and former playboy who suffers a severe chest injury during a kidnapping. His captors attempt to force him to build a weapon of mass destruction, but he instead creates a powered suit of armor to save his life and escape captivity.
Abilities: Genius-level intellect, proficient scientist and engineer. Wears advanced suits of powered armor granting superhuman strength, durability, flight, and a variety of offensive weaponry (repulsor rays, missiles, lasers). Suits are constantly upgraded with new technologies like AI assistants (J.A.R.V.I.S., F.R.I.D.A.Y.).
Affiliations: Founding member of the Avengers, Stark Industries CEO.

Brief:
Iron Man is Tony Stark, a genius inventor who built a high-tech suit of armor to escape captivity and later uses it to fight crime and protect the world, often as a leading member of the Avengers.
