Character: The Flash (DC Comics - Primarily Barry Allen / Wally West)

Details:
Real Name(s): Barry Allen (most common modern), Wally West (successor/alternate), Jay Garrick (original)
Origin (Barry Allen): A police scientist working in his lab when a lightning bolt strikes a shelf of chemicals, dousing him. This accident grants him access to the "Speed Force," an extradimensional energy source giving him superhuman speed and related abilities.
Abilities: Superhuman speed (running, thinking, reacting), capable of vibrating molecules to phase through objects, can travel through time (with difficulty/risk), accelerated healing, generates a protective speed aura. The extent of abilities depends on connection to the Speed Force.
Affiliations: Justice League, Central City Police Department (as Barry Allen), Flash Family.

Brief:
The Flash is a superhero mantle, most famously held by Barry Allen, known for incredible superhuman speed derived from the Speed Force. He uses his powers to fight crime, often in Central City, and is a key member of the Justice League, known as the "Fastest Man Alive."
